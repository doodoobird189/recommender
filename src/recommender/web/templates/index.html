    How it works

    1. Collaborative filtering. Implicit-feedback ALS learns a 64-d vector per movie from who watched what. Your picks are folded in to get your own vector instantly, without retraining.
    2. Content. Each movie has 1,128 tag-genome relevance scores ("dark", "time travel", "witty"…). Your picks form a taste profile, and movies are scored by cosine similarity to it.
    3. Hybrid. Both scores are rescaled so their top picks sit at 1 (raw ALS scores are heavy-tailed and would drown out content), then blended by the slider. Content lets niche and new movies surface even with little rating history. The default blend was tuned on a validation window, never on test data.
    4. Explanations. ALS scores decompose exactly into per-movie contributions, which gives the "Because you liked…" line. Shared tags come from the genome.
    Data: MovieLens (GroupLens). Posters: TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
